# Reference provenance and restrictions

The exact historical references are reused without modifying the split or reference answers.
ACI-Bench data: Wen-wai Yim, Yujuan Fu, Asma Ben Abacha, Neal Snider, Thomas Lin and Meliha Yetisgen (2023), Scientific Data 10:586, doi:10.1038/s41597-023-02487-3. All references are source-first, single-author/automatically assisted. No independent clinician adjudication. Prior results were already inspected: this reused holdout is retrospective and is not a new blind test.
No source interview, real patient narrative, identity mapping or private model trace is included.
New authored probes are research fixtures; released with the author’s authorization. Do not treat public source availability as permission to redistribute the entire parent repository.
